## Music with style and meaning

Michael Sean Miller is a Sacramento-based professional guitarist, wedding host and DJ entertainer with a lifelong passion for creating meaningful musical experiences at live events.

With a mastery of classical and Spanish guitar, Michael has performed at more than 500 events, including some of Northern California's finest restaurants, wineries, ranches and wedding venues, bringing technical artistry and genuine warmth to every show.

## Live performance

- **Live guitar.** Classical and Spanish guitar performed with soul and precision, for restaurant residencies, ceremony soundtracks, cocktail hours and private dinners.
- **Wedding host & MC.** The whole wedding day, ceremony to last dance: introductions, toasts and the first dance, guided with warmth and professionalism.
- **DJ entertainment.** From the first dance to the last song, with live guitar woven into the set.
- **Family & community events.** DJ Gnomie Homie, the garden dance party for the little ones: music, games, bubbles and dancing.

Upcoming shows are always on the [website](https://michaelseanmiller.com).

## Guitar lessons

Guitar lessons for all ages and skill levels, beginners to advanced, with personalized instruction at **The Nicholson Music Co. in Folsom**.

Start with an M.A.P. Assessment (Musical Assessment & Progression) to discover your strengths, explore your goals and receive a personalized roadmap for your musical journey. Specialty instruments are guitar, ukulele, bass and piano, with beginning voice, cello, violin and trumpet also offered.
